Research Summary研究概览

Robust, multimodal, and cross-subject brain decoding鲁棒、多模态、跨个体的脑机解码

Recent work focuses on aligning fMRI responses with visual and semantic representations under realistic constraints: limited new-subject data, strong individual differences, and unavailable source-domain data.

近期工作关注在真实约束下对齐 fMRI 响应与视觉/语义表征：新被试数据有限、个体差异显著、源域原始数据可能不可见。

Cross-subject generalization for fMRI visual decoding.面向 fMRI 视觉解码的跨被试泛化。

Source-free and few-shot adaptation for new subjects.面向新被试的源域不可见与少样本适配。

Multimodal alignment among brain signals, images, text, and semantics.脑信号、图像、文本与语义之间的多模态对齐。

Brain-to-image retrieval, reconstruction, and semantic understanding.脑信号到图像检索、重建与语义理解。
