About方向简介

Brain decoding for generalizable BCI面向可泛化脑机接口的脑解码

Our recent work studies how to align neural activity with visual and semantic representations under realistic constraints: limited new-subject data, strong inter-subject variability, and unavailable source-domain data.

我们的近期工作关注在真实约束下将脑活动与视觉/语义表征对齐：新被试数据有限、个体差异显著、源域原始数据可能不可见。

The homepage collects paper entries, code repositories, project pages, issue feedback links, and benchmark placeholders in one maintainable static site.

该主页集中维护论文条目、代码仓库、项目主页、Issue 反馈入口以及 benchmark/资源占位，便于后续扩展。

Research Directions研究方向

Generalizable neural decoding可泛化神经解码

Recent work is organized around robust adaptation, multimodal alignment, and visual-semantic reconstruction.

近期工作围绕鲁棒适配、多模态对齐以及视觉-语义重建展开。

Cross-Subject Brain Decoding跨个体脑机解码

Generalizable decoding across subjects with different neural response distributions.关注不同被试之间的脑信号分布差异和泛化能力。

Source-Free and Few-Shot Adaptation源域不可见与少样本适配

Rapid adaptation to a new subject when only limited fMRI data are available.关注新被试仅有少量脑信号数据时的快速适配。

Multimodal Neural Representation Learning多模态神经表征学习

Unified modeling between fMRI, EEG, MEG, images, text, and semantics.关注 fMRI、EEG、MEG、图像、文本和语义之间的统一建模。

Brain-to-Image and Brain-to-Semantics Reconstruction脑信号到图像与语义重建

Visual stimulus reconstruction, retrieval, and semantic understanding from brain signals.关注视觉刺激重建、检索和语义理解。
