Duala

Duala: Dual-Level Alignment of Subjects and Stimuli for Cross-Subject fMRI DecodingDuala：面向跨被试 fMRI 解码的被试与刺激双层对齐方法

Shumeng Li, Jintao Guo, Jian Zhang, Yulin Zhou, Luyang Cao, Yinghuan Shi

CVPR 2026 / arXiv:2603.07625 · 2026

Figure图示 Duala

Duala method overview: stimulus-level semantic preservation and subject-level distribution perturbation.Duala 方法框架：刺激层语义保持与受试者层分布扰动。

Overview工作简介

What this work studies这项工作关注什么

Duala targets the core bottleneck of cross-subject fMRI visual decoding: adapting a pretrained decoder to a new subject when only a very small amount of fMRI data is available. The method preserves stimulus semantics while aligning subject-specific neural representations, enabling more stable retrieval and reconstruction under limited adaptation data.Duala 聚焦跨受试者 fMRI 视觉解码中的核心瓶颈：当新受试者只有极少量 fMRI 数据时，如何将预训练解码模型稳定适配到该受试者。该方法在适配个体脑表征差异的同时保持刺激语义结构，从而在有限适配数据下获得更稳定的检索与重建结果。

Accepted by IEEE/CVF CVPR 2026.论文被 IEEE/CVF CVPR 2026 接收。

Addresses expensive and hard-to-scale fMRI collection for new subjects.面向新受试者 fMRI 数据采集昂贵、难以规模化的应用痛点。

Combines stimulus-level semantic preservation with subject-level distribution perturbation.结合刺激层语义保持与受试者层分布扰动，实现双层对齐。

Improves cross-subject visual decoding by reducing semantic confusion during adaptation.通过减少适配过程中的语义混淆，提升跨受试者视觉解码稳定性。

01

Motivation研究动机

fMRI visual decoding aims to reconstruct or identify visual stimuli from brain activity. Recent single-subject decoders have benefited from foundation models such as CLIP and diffusion models, but cross-subject generalization remains difficult because neural anatomy and functional representations vary substantially across individuals.fMRI 视觉解码旨在从脑活动中重建或识别视觉刺激，是理解人类视觉表征机制、推动脑机接口应用的重要方向。近年来，CLIP、扩散模型等基础模型推动了单受试者解码的进展，但由于不同受试者在脑结构和功能表征上存在显著差异，跨受试者泛化仍然十分困难。

02

Challenge核心挑战

Collecting large-scale high-quality fMRI data for every new subject is expensive and hard to scale. Existing cross-subject methods can work well after pretraining, but adaptation to a new subject may disturb the learned semantic structure and blur category boundaries, causing degraded retrieval and reconstruction.为每位新受试者采集大规模高质量 fMRI 数据成本高昂，限制了真实场景中的可扩展性。宣传稿进一步指出，现有跨受试者方案在预训练阶段表现良好，但在新受试者适应微调时容易破坏原有语义结构，使类别边界变得模糊，进而导致检索与重建性能下降。

03

Method方法思路

Duala introduces a dual-level alignment framework. At the stimulus level, semantic alignment and relational consistency constraints preserve the geometry of visual categories across subjects. At the subject level, a distribution perturbation mechanism uses source-subject statistics to guide new-subject representations, reducing overfitting while adapting to individual differences.Duala 提出双层对齐的跨被试解码框架。一方面，通过刺激层的语义约束与关系一致性约束，在不同受试者之间保持类别语义几何结构稳定；另一方面，通过受试者层的分布扰动机制，利用源受试者统计信息引导新受试者表征对齐，在适配个体差异的同时抑制过拟合风险。

04

Results效果展示

By combining stimulus-level consistency with subject-level alignment, Duala preserves semantic structure during adaptation and improves the robustness of cross-subject decoding. The reconstruction examples show that Duala better reflects the semantic categories of the original stimuli and reduces cross-category confusion compared with previous methods.通过结合刺激层一致性和被试层对齐，Duala 能够在适应个体差异的同时保留语义结构，实现更鲁棒、更可泛化的跨被试解码。宣传稿中的重建示例显示，相较既有方法，Duala 生成的图像更准确地反映刺激物语义类别，并有效减少误分类和跨类别混淆。
