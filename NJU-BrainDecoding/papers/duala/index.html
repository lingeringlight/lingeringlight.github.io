Research introduction成果介绍

Duala studies cross-subject fMRI visual decoding, a setting where a decoder trained on existing subjects must be adapted to a new subject with only a small amount of fMRI data. This problem is central to scalable brain decoding because collecting large-scale, high-quality fMRI recordings for every new participant is expensive and difficult to repeat in real applications.Duala 面向跨受试者 fMRI 视觉解码任务，研究如何在新受试者只有少量 fMRI 数据的情况下，将已有受试者上训练得到的解码模型稳定迁移到新受试者。这个问题直接关系到脑解码方法的可扩展性，因为为每位新受试者重新采集大规模、高质量 fMRI 数据成本高昂，也难以在真实应用中反复进行。

Recent fMRI-to-image decoding methods benefit from visual-language models and generative models, but cross-subject adaptation remains challenging. Neural responses differ across individuals, and direct fine-tuning on limited new-subject data can disturb the semantic structure learned during pretraining. As a result, the adapted model may blur category boundaries and produce retrieval or reconstruction results with semantic confusion.近年来，视觉语言模型和生成模型推动了 fMRI 到图像解码的发展，但跨受试者适配仍然具有挑战性。不同受试者的脑结构和功能表征存在显著差异，直接使用少量新受试者数据进行微调，容易破坏预训练阶段已经形成的语义结构，使类别边界变得模糊，从而导致检索或重建结果出现语义混淆。

The core idea of Duala is dual-level alignment. At the stimulus level, the method preserves semantic consistency and relational structure among visual categories, so that adaptation does not collapse the geometry of stimulus representations. At the subject level, Duala introduces distribution perturbation guided by source-subject statistics, helping the new-subject representation move toward a more reliable decoding space while reducing overfitting to scarce adaptation samples.Duala 的核心思想是双层对齐。在刺激层面，方法通过语义约束和关系一致性约束保持视觉类别之间的结构，使适配过程不会破坏刺激表征的语义几何关系；在受试者层面，Duala 引入由源受试者统计信息引导的分布扰动机制，帮助新受试者表征向更可靠的解码空间靠近，同时降低少量适配样本带来的过拟合风险。

By combining stimulus-level semantic preservation with subject-level representation alignment, Duala improves the robustness of cross-subject visual decoding under limited adaptation data. The reconstruction examples show that the decoded images better match the semantic category of the original stimuli and reduce cross-category confusion compared with previous approaches.通过同时考虑刺激层语义保持和受试者层表征对齐，Duala 在有限适配数据下提升了跨受试者视觉解码的鲁棒性。重建结果显示，相较既有方法，Duala 生成的图像更准确地对应原始视觉刺激的语义类别，并减少了跨类别混淆。

This work was completed by the Nanjing University Brain-Computer Interface Institute team and has been accepted by IEEE/CVF CVPR 2026. Shumeng Li is the first author and Prof. Yinghuan Shi is the corresponding author.该工作由南京大学脑机接口研究院团队完成，并被 IEEE/CVF CVPR 2026 接收。博士生李姝萌为论文第一作者，史颖欢教授为论文通讯作者。
