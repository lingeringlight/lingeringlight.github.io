Overview工作简介

What this work studies这项工作关注什么

Dual-level subject and stimulus alignment for cross-subject fMRI decoding with about one hour of adaptation data.利用约 1 小时新被试数据，进行刺激层面与被试层面的双层对齐以提升跨被试 fMRI 解码。

Duala aligns both stimuli and subjects for cross-subject visual decoding, preserving semantic consistency while adapting to individual neural representations with limited fMRI data.Duala 面向跨被试视觉解码，同时建模刺激层面和被试层面对齐，在少量 fMRI 数据下保持语义一致性并适配个体脑表征。
